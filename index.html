05Decided Nov 4

The hackathon, in slow motion.

You suggested a quarter-long build with team accountability. We kept the build-up and added protected time to build. Feb 28 is the backup date.

Where we landed

Multi-day build, Sun Feb 21

  • Shape. Kickoff early in week 7, a couple of build sessions, then a full build day. Optional follow-up with technical support for teams who want to keep going.
  • Projects. Open-ended. Teams build whatever they want, like last year.
  • Why Feb 21. One of the few February weekend days with no conflict, and the Saturday before is free too. Early enough that students have something to show in interviews.
  • Risk. Weekend turnout, and less time to recruit judges than a March date.
